EVERYHOUSEHOLDSHOP WEBSITE SOURCE

Contents
- index.html: storefront and product catalog
- contact.html: contact page
- assets/styles.css: responsive visual design and animations
- assets/app.js: filtering, product bag, localStorage, photo previews, and optional ambient audio

Quick start
1. Extract this ZIP, keeping the assets folder beside both HTML files.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser.
3. The product bag is saved in that browser's local storage. The package has no checkout or login.

Notes
- Product and editorial photos load from Unsplash/Pexels URLs and need an internet connection. Replace these with your final product photography before launch.
- The contact form opens the visitor's email app. Replace hello@everyhouseholdshop.com with your actual contact email.
- The optional ambient sound starts only after the visitor presses Play track.
